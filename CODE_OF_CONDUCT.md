# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community. Participants should communicate professionally, assume good intent, focus on ideas rather than individuals, and welcome different perspectives and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable. Do not publish private information or intentionally interfere with another contributor’s work.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository’s supported contact channel. Include enough context to investigate, and avoid publicizing sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where practical, and take appropriate action, including warnings, content removal, or restriction of participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, issue discussions, pull requests, reviews, and community interactions representing Yolnoma. By participating, you agree to follow these standards and cooperate with reasonable requests from maintainers.
